SPACE ELEVATORS

A space elevator is a concept that is am a long tube or connection that goes from the earth – located around the equator – until it can reach a geostationary orbit - around 35,786km. This is when the structure orbits with a period equal to one day and has a rotation rate equal to the rotation rate of the earth’s surface meaning to a terrestrial observer the object appears stationary.

Its structure will consist of a base station in which people can travel on earth to it. This space station also could be on water which might even have provide use in terms of high-altitude support and control. At the top, however, will have a station which will be the counterweight or centre of mass for the elevator, and thus would require large reels to alter the positioning.
Furthermore, it would have a long cord or tether, which would be used to connect the two together. On this cord, there will be an electromagnetically propelled vehicle. Due to the sheer size of the structure, it would also be required to travel at thousands of kilometres per hour This must be big enough to transport the cargo and the passengers together to the station.
